Decoding Life: From Genes to Generations

Grade: MYC1 | Integrated Science & Mathematics Assessment

Time Allowed: 40 Mins | Total Marks: 25
Scenario Context: Deep inside the Island Wildlife Conservation Research Center, Maya and Rahul work as research assistants on an environmental monitoring project[cite: 1]. Their goal is to investigate how native species adapt, how cellular instructions dictate physical traits, and how mathematical models help track conservation data[cite: 1].
Section 1: Genetics, Adaptation & Biology
1. Where are chromosomes located inside a cell, and what chemical substance are genes made of? [1 Mark]
2. Explain the structural relationship between chromosomes, genes, and DNA in controlling an organism's physical characteristics. [2 Marks]
3. Classify each of the following traits as either Inherited (Genetic) Variation or Environmental Variation:
a) Blood groups
b) A scar on a fox's leg caused by sharp wire
c) Eye color variation in mammals
d) A plant growing smaller due to low soil nutrients or lack of sunlight [2 Marks]
4. A population of wild rabbits shifted from light-colored fur to dark-colored fur after a forest fire darkened the terrain. Describe the process of natural selection that caused this change over generations. [3 Marks]
Section 2: Fractions, Ratios & Direct Proportion
5. (a) Maya prepares a liquid for studying plant cells using 3/4 of a litre. Rahul needs 2/3 of this amount for his experiment. How much liquid does Rahul need? Give your fraction in simplest form.
(b) The team has 7/8 of a litre of reagent. Each sample tube holds 1/16 of a litre. How many sample containers can they fill? [4 Marks]
6. Researchers record 48 total animals from Species A and Species B in the ratio 5 : 3.
a) Calculate the exact number of animals belonging to each species.
b) How many more animals belong to Species A than Species B? [3 Marks]
7. Maya investigates seed germination. The number of seedlings grown (S) is directly proportional to the number of seed trays (T).
[Trays (T): 2 -> Seedlings (S): 14 | Trays (T): 4 -> Seedlings (S): 28 | Trays (T): 6 -> Seedlings (S): 42]
a) Calculate the constant of proportionality, k.
b) Write a formula connecting S and T.
c) How many seedlings will be produced using 11 trays? [3 Marks]
8. Quick Mathematics Skills:
a) Expand and simplify: 3(x + 4)
b) Two angles on a straight line are 3x and 60°. Find x.
c) Simplify the ratio 24 : 36 fully.
d) Calculate: -12 + 7 [4 Marks]
