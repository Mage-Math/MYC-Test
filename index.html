Fractions, Ratios & Direct Proportion

Time Allowed: 40 Mins | Total Marks: 20
1. Multiply: 3/4 × 8/9 [2 Marks]
2. Divide: 5/6 ÷ 2/3 [2 Marks]
3. Simplify the ratio 24 : 36 to its simplest form. [1 Mark]
4. Share $150 in the ratio 2 : 3. What is the larger share? [2 Marks]
5. Three friends share $240 in the ratio 1 : 3 : 4. How much does the middle person get? [2 Marks]
6. $y$ is directly proportional to $x$. When $x = 4$, $y = 20$. Write the formula for $y$ in terms of $x$. [2 Marks]
7. Given $y = 3x$ (direct proportion), find $y$ when $x = 8$. [1 Mark]
