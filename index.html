Fractions, Ratios, Sharing & Direct Proportion

Time: 40 Mins | Total Marks: 25
Instructions: Show all working out clearly for full marks. Use the mathematical symbol toolbar above each text box to easily insert mathematical operators and fraction templates into your response.
1. Work out 3/4 × 8/9. Give your answer as a fraction in its simplest form. [2 Marks]
2. Work out 1 2/3 ÷ 5/6. Show your working and state your answer as a whole number or mixed fraction. [3 Marks]
3. Simplify the ratio 42 : 56 : 70 fully. [2 Marks]
4. Share $280 in the ratio 3 : 4 : 7. Calculate the value of each share. [3 Marks]
5. Alex and Ben share money in the ratio 2 : 5. If Ben receives $45 more than Alex, how much money was shared in total? [3 Marks]
6. Quantity y is directly proportional to x. When x = 6, y = 42. Find the constant of proportionality k and write the formula linking y and x. [3 Marks]
7. Variable P is directly proportional to the square of r (P ∝ r²). When r = 3, P = 45. Find the value of P when r = 5. [4 Marks]
8. A recipe for 8 biscuits requires 120g of flour and 60g of butter. Calculate how much flour and butter are needed to make 20 biscuits. [3 Marks]
