Grade 8 Mathematics Practice Assessment

Fractions, Ratios, Proportions, Algebra & Basic Skills

Question 1 of 10
Question 1: Fraction Operations [4 Marks]
a) A container holds 5/6 of a litre of water. A chemist uses 3/5 of this amount for a mixture. How much water is used? Express your answer in simplest fraction form. [2 Marks]
b) A lab has 9/10 of a litre of solution. Each test vial requires 3/20 of a litre. How many vials can be completely filled? [2 Marks]
Question 2: Basic Fraction Arithmetic [4 Marks]
a) Calculate: 2/5 + 1/3. Give your answer in simplest form. [2 Marks]
b) Calculate: 7/8 - 3/4. [2 Marks]
Question 3: Ratio Sharing [3 Marks]
A sanctuary cares for 72 rescue animals consisting of birds and mammals in the ratio 4 : 5.
a) Calculate how many birds and how many mammals are in the sanctuary. [2 Marks]
b) How many more mammals than birds are there? [1 Mark]
Question 4: Direct Proportion Formula [3 Marks]
The mass of solar panels (M kg) installed is directly proportional to the total electrical power generated (P kW).
[Panels Mass (M): 30 kg → Power (P): 120 kW | Panels Mass (M): 50 kg → Power (P): 200 kW]
a) Calculate the constant of proportionality, k, where P = kM. [1 Mark]
b) Write the formula connecting P and M. [1 Mark]
c) How much power is generated if 85 kg of solar panels are installed? [1 Mark]
Question 5: Mixed Fraction Division [3 Marks]
Work out 2 1/4 ÷ 3/8. Show all working steps and state your final answer as a whole number or mixed fraction.
Question 6: Advanced Ratio Problem [3 Marks]
Clara and David share profit from a project in the ratio 3 : 7. If David receives 120 more than Clara, calculate the total amount of profit shared.
Question 7: Percentages & Conversions [4 Marks]
a) Convert 3/5 into a percentage. [1 Mark]
b) Calculate 15% of 160. [1 Mark]
c) A coat costs 80. Its price is reduced by 20% in a sale. Calculate the new sale price. [2 Marks]
Question 8: Basic Linear Equations [4 Marks]
a) Solve for x: 3x - 5 = 16 [2 Marks]
b) Solve for y: 2(y + 4) = 18 [2 Marks]
Question 9: Non-Linear Direct Proportion [4 Marks]
Variable Y is directly proportional to the square of x (Y ∝ x²). When x = 4, Y = 80. Find the value of Y when x = 6.
Question 10: Core Algebra, Arithmetic & Geometry [5 Marks]
a) Expand and simplify: 4(2x - 3) [1 Mark]
b) Two angles on a straight line are 4x and 40°. Find the value of x. [1 Mark]
c) Simplify the ratio 32 : 48 to its simplest form. [1 Mark]
d) Calculate: -15 + 9 [1 Mark]
e) A rectangle has a length of 14 cm and a width of 6 cm. Calculate its area. [1 Mark]
