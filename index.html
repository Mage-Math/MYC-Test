Mathematics Practice Assessment

Fractions, Ratios, Sharing & Direct Proportion

Time Allowed: 40 Mins | Total Marks: 25
Instructions: Show all working out clearly for full marks. Use the mathematical symbol toolbar above each text area to insert operators, fractions, ratios, and powers into your working.
1. (a) A container holds 5/6 of a litre of water. A chemist uses 3/5 of this amount for a mixture. How much water is used? Express your answer in simplest fraction form.

(b) A lab has 9/10 of a litre of solution. Each test vial requires 3/20 of a litre. How many vials can be completely filled? [4 Marks]
2. A sanctuary cares for 72 rescue animals consisting of birds and mammals in the ratio 4 : 5.

a) Calculate how many birds and how many mammals are in the sanctuary.
b) How many more mammals than birds are there? [3 Marks]
3. The mass of solar panels ($M$ kg) installed is directly proportional to the total electrical power generated ($P$ kW).
[Panels Mass ($M$): 30 kg $\rightarrow$ Power ($P$): 120 kW | Panels Mass ($M$): 50 kg $\rightarrow$ Power ($P$): 200 kW]

a) Calculate the constant of proportionality, $k$, where $P = kM$.
b) Write the formula connecting $P$ and $M$.
c) How much power is generated if 85 kg of solar panels are installed? [3 Marks]
4. Work out 2 1/4 ÷ 3/8. Show all working steps and state your final answer as a whole number or mixed fraction. [3 Marks]
5. Clara and David share profit from a project in the ratio 3 : 7. If David receives $120 more than Clara, calculate the total amount of profit shared. [3 Marks]
6. Variable $Y$ is directly proportional to the square of $x$ ($Y \propto x^2$). When $x = 4$, $Y = 80$. Find the value of $Y$ when $x = 6$. [4 Marks]
7. Core Algebra, Arithmetic & Geometry Skills:
a) Expand and simplify: $4(2x - 3)$
b) Two angles on a straight line are $4x$ and $40^\circ$. Find the value of $x$.
c) Simplify the ratio 32 : 48 to its simplest form.
d) Calculate: $-15 + 9$
e) A rectangle has a length of $14\text{ cm}$ and a width of $6\text{ cm}$. Calculate its area. [5 Marks]
